Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 8, 2026

LABA is an offline audiobook player. This policy explains what limited data is collected and why.

What LABA does NOT collect

  • No account or login — you are never identified by name, email, or any personal identifier.
  • No Advertising ID — the Android Advertising ID (GAID) is explicitly disabled in this app.
  • No microphone, camera, contacts, or location access.
  • Your audiobook files remain entirely on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

Crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics)

LABA uses Firebase Crashlytics (by Google) to automatically detect and report app crashes. When a crash occurs, the following information is sent to Google's servers:

  • Device model, Android version, and available RAM/storage at the time of the crash.
  • App version and whether the app was in the foreground or background.
  • The crash stack trace (code location of the error).
  • A non-identifiable Firebase Installation ID that resets on reinstall.

This data is used exclusively to find and fix bugs. No advertising ID or personal information is included in crash reports.

Anonymous usage analytics (Firebase Analytics)

LABA uses Firebase Analytics (by Google) to understand how many people use the app and on which types of devices. The data collected is:

  • Total number of active users (daily / monthly).
  • Device model, screen size, Android version, and app version.
  • Country (inferred from IP address; the IP itself is not stored by Google long-term).
  • App open and session events (start, end, duration).

No custom events are tracked. No Advertising ID is used. Automatic screen reporting is disabled. This data cannot be used to identify you personally.

Data sharing

Crash reports and analytics data are processed by Google LLC under their Firebase privacy terms. No data is shared with any other third party.

Data retention and deletion

Firebase retains analytics data for up to 14 months by default. Crash reports are retained for 90 days. You may request deletion of data linked to your Firebase Installation ID by contacting the developer (see below); reinstalling the app also resets the Installation ID.

Children

LABA is not directed at children under 13. No data is knowingly collected from children.

Changes to this policy

If the data practices described here change materially, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date revised. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
